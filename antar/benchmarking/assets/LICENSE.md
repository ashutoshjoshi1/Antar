# Benchmark sample images

`shapes.png`, `bar-chart.png`, `traffic-light.png`, `document.png`, and `diagram.png` are
generated by
`scripts/generate_benchmark_images.py`. They contain no third-party content and are
dedicated to the public domain under
[CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

The reference answers used to score the `vision_reference` and `ocr_basic` workloads are
the constants that script draws (colours, counts, and the text in `DOCUMENT_LINES` and
`DIAGRAM_BOXES`), so they are true of the images by construction.
